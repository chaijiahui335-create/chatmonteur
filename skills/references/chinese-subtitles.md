# Chinese Short-Form Caption Rules

## Goal

Readable on a phone, synchronized to meaning, and visually restrained.

## Rules

- Segment by phrase and meaning, not arbitrary character counts.
- Keep line lengths short enough for mobile viewing.
- Avoid covering eyes, mouth, hands demonstrating an object, or important B-roll.
- Use emphasis on keywords, not every word.
- Keep typography consistent across the edit.
- Prefer one clear primary caption treatment instead of many competing styles.
- Use karaoke timing only when it helps speech rhythm.
- Avoid giant captions that dominate the entire frame unless the phrase is intentionally a title card.
- Avoid repeated caption content when a graphic already communicates the same phrase.

## Emphasis

Prioritize emphasis for:

- the core problem
- the surprising contrast
- the key number
- the conclusion
- the call to action

Do not emphasize ordinary connector words.

## Safety check

After rendering, inspect caption placement at 9:16 mobile scale. If the caption makes the speaker's expression or a meaningful visual hard to read, reposition or shorten it.
