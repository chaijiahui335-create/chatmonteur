# Speaker Cutout / Masking Rules

## Purpose

Use a real speaker cutout as a temporary composition tool for explanation and emphasis.

## Preferred compositions

1. Speaker foreground + simple contextual background
2. Speaker on one side + screenshot/photo/number card on the other
3. Speaker cutout overlapping a relevant visual when the overlap improves hierarchy

## Requirements

- Preserve natural facial expression and lip visibility.
- Keep edges clean enough at 9:16 output resolution.
- Do not use cutout continuously throughout the whole video.
- Do not place important text directly over the face.
- Keep background graphics simple enough that the speaker remains the visual anchor.

## Trigger

Use masking when the spoken content benefits from showing a second visual while retaining the speaker's authority/presence. Do not trigger it solely because a cutout effect is available.
