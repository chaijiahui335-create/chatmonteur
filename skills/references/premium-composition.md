# Premium Composition System

## Objective

Make a vertical talking-head edit feel intentionally art-directed rather than template-driven.

## Composition states

Use a small vocabulary of stable layouts and transition between them with purpose:

- `A`: clean speaker full frame
- `B`: speaker tighter crop + emphasis text
- `C`: speaker + product/image split
- `D`: speaker cutout + contextual background
- `E`: evidence/screenshot full frame
- `F`: product/detail close-up
- `G`: simple statement card

## State transitions

Prefer hard cuts, clean reveals, or subtle motion. Do not transition for every sentence.

Avoid:

- constant bouncing text
- random wipes
- heavy glow effects
- excessive borders and cards
- decorative stickers with no meaning
- identical templates repeated across the whole video

## Visual hierarchy

One primary focal point per shot. Secondary information must have lower visual weight.

Keep faces, products, and important text away from unsafe edges and UI-covered regions in vertical platforms.

## Rhythm

Use composition changes at semantic boundaries: new point, example, contrast, evidence, conclusion. Preserve stable shots for emotional or credibility-heavy delivery.

## Brand neutrality

Do not invent a brand color system. Use restrained typography and neutral graphic treatment until project-specific branding is provided.
