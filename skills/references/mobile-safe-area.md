# 9:16 Mobile Safe Area

## Purpose

Keep important information visible and readable on short-video apps where interface controls can cover the frame.

## Rules

- Keep key captions inside a conservative central safe area.
- Do not place critical text directly at the extreme top or bottom.
- Protect eyes, mouth, hands demonstrating a product, and important product details from caption overlap.
- Recheck every composition at actual vertical phone scale, not only on a desktop preview.
- If a graphic and captions compete for the same space, simplify the graphic or move the captions.
